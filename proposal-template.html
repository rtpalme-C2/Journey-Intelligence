French Quarter · New Orleans, LA
Where to Stay
Two strong options — one puts you in the middle of everything, one pulls you just far enough away.
Both hotels are well-established French Quarter options with strong reputations. Monteleone puts you inside the Quarter steps from Bourbon Street; Windsor Court sits a half-block from Canal — quieter, more upscale, and slightly removed from the noise.
Where to Stay
Hotel Monteleone
A French Quarter landmark since 1886, on Royal Street steps from Bourbon. Home to the famous Carousel Bar — a slowly rotating cocktail bar that's a destination on its own. Rooftop pool, full-service spa, and 570 rooms with marble bathrooms and plush robes. Couples rate the location 9.8/10.
★ 4.4 · ~$250–$380 / night (weekend) · ~$150–$220 (weekday)
The Windsor Court
Five-star hotel a half-block from Canal Street — close enough to walk everywhere, far enough to feel like a retreat. Rated 9.6/10 (Exceptional). Features $10 million in museum-quality art. Full-service spa, rooftop pool and bar, and The Grill Room — the city's only AAA Four Diamond restaurant.
★ 4.8 · ~$370–$500 / night (weekend) · ~$220–$320 (weekday)
Weekend Itinerary
Friday — Fly in, drop bags, go
Afternoon
Fly DFW to MSY (~1.5 hrs nonstop). Check in, freshen up.
Evening
Dinner at Cafe Amelie — patio seating on a quiet Royal Street courtyard under bistro lights. Reserve ahead; outdoor tables fill quickly. Known for shrimp and grits.
Night
Carousel Bar at Hotel Monteleone — even if you're not staying there, this is a must. Then bar-hop Bourbon Street: Lafitte's Blacksmith Shop for frozen daiquiris, Pat O'Brien's for the courtyard and a Hurricane.
Saturday — Full day in the Quarter
Morning
Brunch at Brennan's — a New Orleans institution since 1946. Famous for Bananas Foster, invented here.
Afternoon
Walk Royal Street for art galleries and antiques. Stroll Jackson Square, watch street performers near St. Louis Cathedral. Stop at Muriel's courtyard for an afternoon cocktail.
Evening
Dinner at Restaurant R'evolution — upscale Creole, intimate atmosphere, extensive cocktail and wine list. Note any occasion when booking.
Night
Preservation Hall for live traditional jazz (shows 8–10 PM — book ahead). Then Frenchmen Street; The Spotted Cat has the best jazz in the city.
Sunday — Easy morning, fly home
Morning
Beignets and cafe au lait at Cafe Du Monde — open-air patio facing Jackson Square. Browse the French Market for hot sauce and pralines.
Midday
Head to the airport for an afternoon flight back to Dallas (~1.5 hrs).

Weekend vs. Weekday Pricing
The food, bars, and experience are identical — the crowds and the cost are not.
New Orleans is a strong case for going mid-week. Hotels drop noticeably Monday–Thursday and flights follow the same pattern. Going weekday saves roughly $350–$600 per couple depending on hotel choice.
Flights — DFW to MSY (Nonstop)
American Airlines operates the most frequent nonstop service (~1 hr 30 min). Weekday departures (Monday/Thursday) typically run $100–$160 per person round trip. Weekend departures push to $180–$280 per person. Budget carriers like Spirit and Frontier occasionally undercut those ranges.
